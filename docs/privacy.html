Privacy Policy

CommonHours

Effective 2 September 2026

Summary

CommonHours runs entirely on your own computer. It has no servers and no operator-side storage. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information with anyone. The only network destinations it contacts are the official APIs of the platforms you have authorized.

1. Who this covers

CommonHours is software you download and run yourself. In this policy, "you" means the person running it. There is no CommonHours company, service, or hosted product that receives your data.

2. What is stored, and where

All of the following is stored only on the computer where you run CommonHours. None of it is transmitted to the author of the software or to any third party.

Authorization tokens

When you connect a TikTok account, the access token and refresh token TikTok issues are written to a file in your home directory (~/.config/socialpost/tokens.json), with filesystem permissions that restrict it to your own user account. These tokens are used only to authenticate your uploads to TikTok.

Posting history

A local file records a cryptographic hash of each video you have posted, the platform, the resulting post ID, and a timestamp. This exists so that a repeated or automated run cannot publish the same video twice. It contains no personal information.

Your videos and captions

Video files and captions stay on your machine until you choose to post, at which point they are uploaded to the platform you selected. CommonHours keeps no copy beyond the file you already had.

3. Information received from TikTok

Before each post, CommonHours calls TikTok's Creator Info endpoint. TikTok returns your account nickname and username, the privacy levels available to your account, whether comments, duet and stitch are enabled, and your maximum allowed video duration.

This information is used immediately, in memory, to build a valid post request and to respect your account settings. It is displayed to you in your terminal and is not written to disk, retained after the program exits, or sent anywhere.

4. What is never done

  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No TikTok content or account data is scraped, indexed, aggregated, or republished elsewhere.
  • No content is posted to any account other than one you have explicitly authorized on your own machine.
  • No advertising or automated decision-making is performed on your data.

5. This website

These pages are static files served by GitHub Pages. They set no cookies, run no analytics, and load no third-party trackers. GitHub may log requests to its own servers as described in GitHub's privacy statement; that is outside the control of CommonHours.

6. Retention and deletion

Because all data is local, you control it completely. To remove everything:

  • Delete ~/.config/socialpost/tokens.json to remove stored authorization tokens.
  • Delete ~/.local/state/socialpost/posted.json to remove the posting history.
  • Revoke the app's access from your TikTok account settings, under Security and permissions, to invalidate any token that already exists.
  • Delete the program directory to remove the software.

No request to anyone is needed, because no one else holds a copy.

7. Children

CommonHours is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age required by the platforms it connects to.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date. The change history is public in the source repository.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at github.com/Rico36/ShortVideos/issues.
